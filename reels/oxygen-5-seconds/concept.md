# Concept: What if Earth lost oxygen for 5 seconds?

## Logline
Oxygen vanishes for five seconds; the sky goes black, the beaches burn, and every concrete building crumbles — and holding your breath saves no one.

## Style pick
`kinetic-typography` (bang-motion) — stat-driven "What If" science; Kurzgesagt energy via GSAP motion graphics. Motion amplitude must exceed the bang-motion reference (YAVG spread ≥ 20, target ≥ 25).

## Palette (3 colors + neutral)
- Background: `#0B1026` (deep space navy)
- Accent 1: `#FF3B30` (alarm red — oxygen loss, danger)
- Accent 2: `#FFD60A` (sun yellow — heat, ticking clock)
- Neutral text: `#F2F2F7` (near-white)

## Protagonist
Earth itself (stylized flat globe) + a stick-figure human for scale shots. Both renderable as flat SVG shapes. Earth traits: simple circle, 2-3 landmass blobs, thin atmosphere ring. Human: one-line stick figure, 12fps stepped motion.

## Beat sheet (~50s, shift every 2–3s)
| t | Beat | Visual shift |
|---|------|-------------|
| 0–3s | HOOK | Earth from space fills frame → flashes red. Text: "5 SECONDS" slams in. |
| 3–6s | QUESTION | Camera pushes in on Earth; oxygen molecules (small O₂ dots) drain away downward. |
| 6–10s | SUBVERSION | Stick figure holding breath, chest puffed → shakes head NO. Text: "HOLDING YOUR BREATH WON'T SAVE YOU". |
| 10–15s | FACT 1 setup | Sky panel above city turns from blue → pitch black in one snap (no fade). Stars pop in. |
| 15–20s | FACT 1 why | Light-scattering particle diagram: particles vanish, sun ray goes straight, no blue. |
| 20–25s | FACT 2 setup | Split screen: beach left, normal sun right. |
| 25–30s | FACT 2 hit | Beach side sun turns harsh white-yellow, skin tone flashes red (sunburn). Text: "INSTANT SUNBURN". |
| 30–35s | FACT 3 setup | Concrete building corner, solid. |
| 35–40s | FACT 3 hit | Building crumbles to dust pile in stepped 12fps frames. Text: "CONCRETE = DUST". |
| 40–46s | PUNCH | Black screen → Earth reappears, oxygen dots flood back, counter "5.0s" resets. |
| 46–50s | LOOP OUT | Final frame matches hook framing (Earth centered, red flash) → seamless loop. |

## Constraints inherited
- Vertical 1080×1920. No fades. Hard cuts + whip transitions only. Static grain 25%.
- Max 2 text tiers per scene. Clock tick SFX under entire reel (audio director).
