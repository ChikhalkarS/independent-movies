Oppenheimer didn't invent the deadliest weapon... he read about it.

In the Mahabharata, the Brahmastra was no mere arrow. It was a weapon of mass destruction — described with terrifying precision.

Ancient Sanskrit texts detail its effects: environmental devastation. Infertility of the land for generations. Genetic damage passed through bloodlines. Radiation sickness before radiation was known.

The parallels to atomic theory are undeniable. Thermal radiation. Blast wave. Fallout. Half-life contamination.

But the texts also describe its containment. A mystical shield — a golden dome of energy — that absorbed the explosion. An ancient safety protocol for a weapon that could end the world.

Oppenheimer quoted the Gita: "Now I am become Death." He knew. The knowledge was always there.

The Brahmastra returns to dormancy. The counter resets. The loop begins again.
